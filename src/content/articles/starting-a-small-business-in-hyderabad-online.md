Taking a business online can feel like a long list of tasks. The process becomes more manageable when you build the essentials in the right order.

## 1. Organise the business information

Prepare your business name, service list, address or service area, phone number, email, hours and a short description. Make sure the information is accurate and approved for public use.

## 2. Establish a recognisable identity

Prepare a usable logo, a small colour palette and consistent typography. Apply them to your sign, visiting card, invoices and online profiles.

## 3. Create a simple website

Start with a clear homepage, service information, about section and contact page. Add genuine photographs and make it easy to call or send an enquiry from a phone.

## 4. Set up local discovery

Create or update relevant business listings, verify ownership and keep details consistent. Encourage genuine customer feedback without scripting or purchasing reviews.

## 5. Prepare a small content library

Photograph your products, premises, people and work. Write short answers to common customer questions. Reuse this material thoughtfully across your website and social channels.

## 6. Review performance

Track calls, enquiries and completed sales. Ask customers how they found you. Improve the pages and channels that help people make informed decisions.

There is no need to launch every digital channel on day one. Build a reliable foundation, keep the details current and add complexity only when the business is ready.

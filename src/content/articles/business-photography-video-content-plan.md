Professional photographs and videos help customers see what a business actually offers. The strongest content plan begins with customer questions, not with a camera shot list.

## Build four useful content groups

1. **Products and services:** show the details, process, results and options.
2. **People:** introduce the team, expertise and the humans behind the business.
3. **Place and process:** show the workspace, equipment, preparation and customer experience.
4. **Proof and stories:** share real projects, demonstrations and customer feedback with permission.

## Plan one shoot around multiple formats

Prepare a shot list before the shoot. Capture wide, medium and close-up views, vertical and horizontal framing where needed, and enough clean footage for short edits. Keep key subjects away from areas likely to be covered by platform interface elements.

## Keep the message specific

A short video should have one main idea. Open with a clear hook, show the relevant detail and close with a useful next step. Avoid adding claims that cannot be substantiated.

## Create a repeatable monthly rhythm

Group similar content into one planned production session. Schedule product details, team introductions, frequently asked questions and project highlights across the month. Review which posts lead to enquiries, saves or meaningful conversations.

Good business content is not about publishing constantly. It is about helping the right audience understand the value of what you do.
